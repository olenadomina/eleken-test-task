# Research findings

Source: Olena's earlier LMS research, 2024 (FigJam wchakjcyAnblEecQuw7NZW; portfolio case olenadiomina.com/work/scale). Learner survey, user and stakeholder interviews, 6 personas, 30+ user stories, competitor usability review. The survey sample size is not stated on the board; percentages are quoted as written there.

## Survey (sticky notes on the board, translated)

- 77% use a laptop to study.
- 67% find it hard to motivate and organize themselves.
- 95% say visualizing progress matters; 73% say tracking progress affects their motivation.
- 67% say deadlines affect their motivation.
- 50% say rewards for learning affect their motivation.
- 92% say interactive elements and tasks matter.
- 77% prefer text communication; 59% prefer video calls for some communication.
- 88% prefer video lectures; 67% interactive sessions.

Reasons for not finishing courses (stickies): no time; low-quality or boring content; no motivation or changed priorities; **no homework review and no feedback**; no deadlines; **negative attitude and demotivating comments from the teacher**; technical problems or inconvenient platform; badly planned course with no clear structure.

## Stakeholder interview (translated quotes)

- "Pain – everything is in different places, many chats you have to scroll through – chaos."
- The learner "must be able to log into their profile, understand which courses they are taking now and at what stage, plan their week, do lessons and homework on time, join calls, see updates."

## User stories ("As a learner, I want…", translated, selected)

- continue from where I stopped, so I don't waste time finding the place
- manage several courses in 1 space to plan my time
- easily submit my homework without spending a lot of time on it
- get clear feedback from the mentor, so I understand my progress
- have concrete deadlines to control my learning
- see a schedule of upcoming lessons to plan my time
- understand where the theory is and where the homework is
- get notified about important events

## Competitor usability review (insights)

- People may not understand what to do with earned badges.
- Clear homework upload, subtitles and progress of watched content raise satisfaction.
- A 4-step unsubscribe flow reads as an anti-wow moment.
