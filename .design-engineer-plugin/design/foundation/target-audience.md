# Target audience

Sources: Eleken brief; persona and survey work from the earlier LMS research (FigJam wchakjcyAnblEecQuw7NZW, sections "Persona 1–6", "Goals and Motivations", "Pain Points and Challenges", "User Survey"); test file Notes (assumptions A1–A3).

## Segment 1 – Learner

Persona 1 from the earlier research (verbatim summary, translated): a woman 30+ who actively uses technology and the internet to grow her career; studies online to get new professional skills; short on time because of work load and work–life balance; needs flexible planning and access to quality courses.

Goals and motivations (earlier research): seeing progress (95% of survey respondents), deadlines (67%), interactive tasks (92%), rewards (50%), a sense of community.

Pain points (earlier research): motivation and self-discipline, missing feedback, missing deadlines, no community, inconvenient interface, slow answers when something breaks.

Context of use: 77% study on a laptop (earlier survey). Opens the platform in the evening after work, has 30–60 minutes, wants to continue where she stopped.

Example user in the prototype: Anna Kovalenko, taking "UX Research Fundamentals", "Figma: Advanced Prototyping" and "Product Analytics for Designers".

## Segment 2 – Admin / Teacher (reviewer)

From the brief: "a teacher / reviewer / admin who needs to review student submissions efficiently across courses and modules."

Assumption A1 (Notes, not yet validated): reviewers work in batches across several courses. Most of their time goes into finding what is urgent. Works on a laptop, reviews in a session of 10–30 submissions.

Example user in the prototype: Kateryna Mudryk ("You"), reviewer on 3 courses; colleagues Marta Koval and Ihor Petrenko.

## Segment 3 – Observer (read-only)

Has access to the courses to read submissions and comments but cannot grade, assign or change status (brief: "read-only" state). Example: a course assistant or an academic lead.
